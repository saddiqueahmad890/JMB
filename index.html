Where is JMB Design & Build located?

Our main office is located at 14 The Service Road, Potters Bar, Hertfordshire, EN6 1QA. We are based in Hertfordshire but proudly serve clients across London, Essex and the surrounding home counties — including St Albans, Barnet, Enfield, Radlett, Shenley, Brookmans Park and many more.

How long have you been in business?

J.M.B Design & Build Ltd was founded by James Boulton, who along with his team of qualified craftsmen has more than 25 years of experience in the construction industry. We have successfully completed projects ranging from loft conversions and extensions to full new builds and Grade II listed building renovations.

What services do you offer?

We offer a full range of design and build services including: House Extensions, Loft Conversions, Garage Conversions, New Builds, Home Renovations, Electrical works and Lighting installations. We also have an in-house Architectural Planning and Design team to help manage planning permissions and regulatory requirements.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes! We offer a free, no-obligation consultation and site visit. Simply get in touch via our contact form or call us on +44 (0) 1707 242892 and we'll arrange a convenient time to visit, take measurements, discuss your vision and provide a detailed quote.
